what is an ai agent sandbox?

an ai agent sandbox is a separate workspace with limits on what the agent can reach or change. it lets you try a task on copies while the originals stay outside.

by the dusk bot team · checked 20 november 2026 · how we made this

the protection depends on what crosses that boundary, including folders you share and accounts you sign into there.

in one line

a sandbox confines an ai agent's work to an area with a defined set of files, tools and permissions.

an example

beverly wants to try adding captions to old family photos. for this practice run, she copies a few pictures into a separate workspace. she keeps the family archive outside it. if the agent puts a cousin's name on the wrong photo, she can discard that copy and try again.

draw the boundary around the originals

ask what the agent could change if it picked the wrong file. can it open the family archive from the workspace? can a signed-in photo account edit the originals online? if either answer is yes, another folder named “practice” offers little protection.

a good trial leaves you with copies you can throw away. compare the captions and filenames before moving anything back.

what dusk cloud keeps separate

dusk cloud gives the bot a desktop separate from yours. each cloud has its own storage. plus and pro each run in their own sandbox. team has a dedicated machine.12

you can share a read-only copy of a local folder with the cloud. the copy stays up to date while your computer is on. files the bot makes come down to documents/dusk bot.1

accounts cross the boundary too

give the bot only the app access its practice task needs. dusk bot lets you turn a connected app off for that bot or allow reading only.3 captioning a handful of copies needs no connection to the family account.

if your rule is that photos must never leave your machine, dusk cloud is the wrong place for the trial.

questions

is a separate browser tab a sandbox for my files?

a new tab alone tells you little about file access. look at which folders the agent can open and which accounts the browser can reach.

will a sandbox make the captions accurate?

no. it limits the consequences of a bad guess. someone who knows the family still needs to review the names.
