what is a background agent?

a background agent works on a task while you turn your attention elsewhere. you might start it once and come back for the result.

by the dusk bot team · checked 20 november 2026 · how we made this

before walking away, agree on what it should leave for you and what should make it stop. switching windows and shutting your laptop have different consequences.

in one line

a background agent is an ai that can work without you following each step as it happens.

an example

gordon wants a list of missing costume sizes before a dress rehearsal. in this example, he leaves an agent comparing the cast list with the fitting sheets while he sets out chairs. one fitting sheet won't open. a useful result distinguishes people with no size on file from those whose sheets it couldn't read.

make a partial result easy to spot

ask for a count of the sheets the agent could actually read. a tidy list of missing sizes can hide a whole unreadable sheet. ask it to name that sheet and leave the affected names unresolved.

if the wardrobe team needs an answer before rehearsal, agree when someone will look at the result. a background task can still get stuck on a question only you can answer.

can you walk away from a dusk bot routine?

for recurring work in dusk bot, pick where the routine will run. on your computer, routines stop when the app closes or the machine sleeps. dusk cloud runs routines on time with your computer off.1

open the routine in the bot's side panel for its recent runs. if you want to follow up from the venue, the phone can pair with dusk cloud. a phone paired with your own computer still depends on that computer running the app.12

when leaving it running buys you little

stay with the task if each missing size needs an immediate fitting. dusk bot can help organize the questions. if the next step always needs you and the performer in the same room, working through the sheets together is simpler.

questions

does background mean the task repeats?

no. comparing today's fitting sheets can be a one-off. use a routine when you also want a time or event to start the work again.

will dusk bot catch up on a missed routine?

on your computer, a routine less than 12 hours late runs once when you return. older runs are skipped. plan around the rehearsal deadline if a late list would be useless.1
