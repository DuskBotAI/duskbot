how to schedule a daily grok task with grok bot

to schedule a daily grok task, give one grok bot a routine with a time, time zone and job. for a morning brief, name the sources and what counts as news.

by the dusk bot team · checked 20 november 2026 · how we made this

you can also start a routine from an event, such as a slack message. the cloud computer runs it even with your devices off.29

pick the grok product that runs your job

use grok bot for the cloud routine you want waiting in the morning. as of 12 november 2026, the tasks page calls its feature automations. it describes recurring work, events and notifications.1

a grok bot routine belongs to a bot. it can follow a schedule or start when an event arrives, such as a slack message or a github issue event.2

grok build is a coding agent. its docs cover interactive terminal work, scripts, bots and acp use in other apps.3

start with the result you need. a public-news brief and a coding script might both repeat weekly. they still need different settings and a different answer to “which computer runs this?”

read the x plan beside the price

the plan names changed on 12 october 2026. xai brought grok, grok bot, the cursor coding app and x premium into the x subscription. new customers no longer buy standalone supergrok; existing subscribers can keep their plan.5

as of 20 november 2026, three x subscription plans include grok bot:10

  • premium: $30 a month
  • super: $100 a month
  • ultra: $200 a month

free and the $8 plus plan exclude grok bot. there's no separate bot subscription.10 paid cursor individual plans and self-serve teams also qualify. see grok bot pricing for legacy subscriptions.9

read the amount at checkout; it may use local currency. the agent cost guide separates product, machine and ai bills.

give the recurring job a question it can finish

a vague weekly task gives you a vague answer every week. write down the source, question, output and when to stay quiet. try one of these shapes:

  • topic watch: review original public transport announcements. separate decisions from speculation. link each source and date. keep quiet when nothing changed.
  • research follow-up: reread the question in a saved brief. explain what new evidence changes the answer and what remains unknown.
  • planning prompt: read this goals file each monday. ask which goal needs a decision before expanding a plan.

test once before scheduling. narrow the sources if the answer wanders. a schedule does not grant access to private accounts.

decide where the work runs before shutting the lid

grok bot runs the routine on its cloud computer. closing the app or switching off your phone or laptop leaves the routine running.9

grok build instead describes scripts, bots and acp. first launch uses browser sign-in; environments without a browser use an api key.3

xai’s consumer terms were last updated 27 october 2026. they forbid sharing credentials or making your account available to others. you remain responsible for its activity. the build docs do not settle every other app’s use of your sign-in.6

check current provider terms. using your existing ai subscription explains dusk’s official-app route alongside those boundaries.

when another routine app earns its place

keep a working grok routine if it already does the job. another app can add setup without adding value.

dusk suits someone who wants each bot’s job, memory, recurring work and follow-up questions together. send a routine request and read its schedule card for the next run. automations in the sidebar shows run history.78

a local dusk routine stops when the computer sleeps or the app closes. dusk cloud runs the routine on time.8

compare grok bot and dusk bot for model choice and hosting, or see other schedulers. choose the home that keeps its sources, results and review in reach.

a tutorial’s product name matters more than the date on its price screenshot.

try it in 2 minutes

  1. read the product and plan names in your account
  2. choose a public topic and a short source list
  3. try a brief with source dates and gaps
  4. confirm which product runs it before scheduling

before trusting a recurring task

  • you know which grok product runs it
  • the plan and checkout price are current
  • the task names its sources and quiet condition
  • you know where results and failures appear

questions

can grok tasks work with my laptop closed?

yes, if it's a grok bot routine. it runs on the cloud computer. a grok build script running on your own laptop needs that laptop awake.93

is supergrok still a separate plan?

new customers use the x subscription as of 12 november 2026. existing supergrok subscribers can keep their plan. read the name on your bill.5

what should i try as my first recurring grok task?

pick a public topic you already follow. ask for dated sources, new evidence and gaps. test that answer before repeating it.

how do i set up a daily grok bot schedule?

assign the routine to the bot that should do the work. write the schedule in plain language, for example “every weekday at 7am london time, summarise today's rail notices for my route.” include the route and source. grok bot supports time zones and requires at least five minutes between runs.2
