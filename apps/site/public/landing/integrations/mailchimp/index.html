a mailchimp bot for the moments before and after a send

find out what would stop a campaign from going out before launch morning. a mailchimp bot in dusk can read the send checklist and the email itself.

by the dusk bot team · checked 20 november 2026 · how we made this

after the send, it can bring back delivery figures and clicks by link. keep the bot on reporting duty while you make the call to send.

what a bot can do in Mailchimp

use the campaign id from the edition you mean. a draft, a test idea and last week’s send can have nearly identical names. have the bot show the title and status before reading the content or reporting numbers.

actiondusk bot treats it aswhat you would use it for
List campaignsreadfind the edition in the right audience and date window
Get campaign inforeadconfirm the chosen campaign and its status
Get campaign send checklistreadseparate errors blocking a send from optional warnings
Get campaign contentreadcompare the plain-text copy with the html version
Get campaign reportreadread delivery and engagement totals for the sent campaign
List campaign click detailsreadrank tracked destinations by the click metric you choose
Get campaign link detailsreadread total and unique clicks for an individual tracked destination

composio, the connection service behind dusk bot's apps, lists 278 Mailchimp actions (version 20261116_00, read 20 november 20264). dusk bot treats 142 as reads, 5 as sends and 131 as changes.

only actions starting with a read word (get, list, search) count as reads, so a read-only bot can't call the rest. a send reaches people or moves money, like add subscriber to workflow email, and waits for your Allow unless you set a daily amount.

connect Mailchimp

  1. open Connected apps in the sidebar.
  2. pick Mailchimp and sign in on Mailchimp's own page, so your password goes to them and never to a bot.
  3. choose which bots may use it, and whether each may only read or may also change things. your bookkeeper doesn't need your Mailchimp.
  4. ask in plain words. reading and drafting happen freely; sending asks you first.

give the reporting bot an exact list of read actions. connect mailchimp, choose your bot and select the reads above. give it the newsletter audience and the campaign id for a recent send. ask for the most-clicked destination before scheduling regular reports.

routines to copy

treat launch prep and results as different jobs. a campaign can pass the send checklist and still have the wrong offer in its copy. use the first two routines before launch and the last one after readers have had a chance to click.

launch blockers

every thursday at 3, find the newest draft for my newsletter audience. read its send checklist. give me the campaign id and the errors i have to fix. put optional warnings on a separate line. leave it as a draft.

thursdays at 3 pm · errors that block launch and warnings that need judgment

offer in both versions

every friday at 9, open the newest newsletter draft. compare the offer, deadline and main destination in its html and plain-text copy. quote any mismatch here with its location. flag either version if it is empty. leave the copy alone.

fridays at 9 am · differences a reader would notice between email versions

where readers went

every monday at noon, find last week’s sent newsletter for my audience. get its report and tracked link details. list delivery totals and rank the destinations by unique clicks. name the metric. separate the main offer from footer links. if nothing went out, skip the ranking.

mondays at noon · delivery figures and a ranked list of destinations

what it asks you first

keep resend off the action list too. send campaign asks first by default. resend campaign can also deliver mail, but dusk classifies that action as a change. the send rule does not cover it. a reporting bot only needs the named reads.

careful with Full access: a bot there with no sending rule can send up to 25 a day without asking. give it Ask every time.

what it cannot do

  • measure a draft’s performance. mailchimp can return zeros for a draft. read the status before treating zero as a result.
  • rank links without tracking data. the click detail action lists tracked links for sent campaigns. a missing link has no figure to rank.
  • explain a reader’s intent from a click. a click shows a destination. it does not tell you whether the reader liked the offer.

deciding if the campaign is ready

reserve the final read for someone who knows the offer. a send checklist can identify a missing requirement. it cannot decide whether the discount in the email matches what you meant to promise. if your only job is to press send on an approved edition, do that in mailchimp.

questions

can i find which newsletter link got the most attention?

choose the click metric first. ask for unique clicks per destination and the campaign id. repeated clicks by one reader can change a ranking based on total clicks.

does the bot need my subscriber list for a weekly report?

campaign totals and link results are enough for these routines. leave individual subscriber lookups off its action list.
