find the decision halfway down a teams thread

bring the decision to the next meeting, even when it sits twelve replies below the original proposal. give a dusk bot the microsoft teams thread and ask who agreed to what.

by the dusk bot team · checked 20 november 2026 · how we made this

it can read replies, compare them with an available meeting transcript and return the lines that settle the question in your dusk chat.

what a bot can do in Microsoft Teams

name the place where people actually answered. a channel post, a chat pin and a meeting transcript each have a different id. start with the one that holds the decision. use the others when the team needs to reconcile two versions.

actiondusk bot treats it aswhat you would use it for
Get Teams channel messagereadopen the proposal at the top of a known channel thread
List channel message repliesreadread what colleagues accepted or changed in later replies
List pinned messagesreadcollect instructions pinned in a particular chat
List meeting transcriptsreadfind transcript records available for the meeting you named
Get meeting transcript contentreadread the words behind a disputed meeting note
Send Channel Messagesend: asks firstpublish a reviewed announcement as a fresh channel post

composio, the connection service behind dusk bot's apps, lists 253 Microsoft Teams actions (version 20261120_00, read 20 november 20264). dusk bot treats 127 as reads, 7 as sends and 119 as changes.

only actions starting with a read word (get, list, search) count as reads, so a read-only bot can't call the rest. a send reaches people or moves money, like reply to teams channel message, and waits for your Allow unless you set a daily amount.

connect Microsoft Teams

  1. open Connected apps in the sidebar.
  2. pick Microsoft Teams and sign in on Microsoft Teams's own page, so your password goes to them and never to a bot.
  3. choose which bots may use it, and whether each may only read or may also change things. your bookkeeper doesn't need your Microsoft Teams.
  4. ask in plain words. reading and drafting happen freely; sending asks you first.

give the bot the team, channel and parent message ids for a recurring discussion. connect microsoft teams in connected apps and allow the named read actions. for a chat pin review, give it the chat id instead. channel replies belong to their parent post. put the planning call’s online meeting id in the bot’s job.

routines to copy

agree on what counts as a decision. some teams use an owner’s yes. others need a date and a named person to act. tell the bot which evidence to bring back.

decisions in the launch thread

at 4 each weekday, open our launch thread in teams. read the original message and every page of replies. list decisions made today, the person who agreed and the line that confirms each one. leave suggestions under open questions here.

weekdays at 4 pm · confirmed decisions with the replies that support them

meeting promises

every thursday at 3, read the available transcript for the planning call whose online meeting id is in your job. pick out promises people made in their own words. show the speaker and deadline when the text gives them. bring me any unclear ownership. do not post minutes.

thursdays at 3 pm · commitments for the meeting organizer to confirm

instructions pinned in chat

every monday at 9, read all pinned messages in our operations chat, including their text. find any that name a date already past. quote the instruction and date here so i can decide whether to keep the pin. leave the pins alone.

mondays at 9 am · pinned instructions with dates worth revisiting

what it asks you first

keep the decision note in dusk until someone chooses its audience. posting a channel message asks first by default. the post action creates a new top-level message. it will not put the note under the proposal. editing an existing channel message is a change and has no send prompt.

careful with Full access: a bot there with no sending rule can send up to 25 a day without asking. give it Ask every time.

what it cannot do

  • retrieve a transcript that the meeting does not have. the transcript content action needs a calendar-backed online meeting with an available transcript.
  • read a whole channel thread from its parent alone. replies have their own action. it can take several pages to reach the last one.
  • start a routine the moment a teams reply lands through this connector. choose a review time or ask in dusk when you need the answer.

when the team still disagrees

a summary cannot break a tie. if the replies contain two different launch dates, give the owner both lines. if you need a decision in a live conversation, ask the people in teams. use the bot to recover the record once the discussion has moved on.

before

questions

will this bot join our meeting to take notes?

the transcript routine reads a transcript after one exists. give it a supported meeting id. if there is no transcript to read, it should say so.

does a pinned message belong to a channel or a chat here?

the pin lookup takes a chat id. give it the operations chat for that routine. a channel thread needs the team, channel and parent message instead.
