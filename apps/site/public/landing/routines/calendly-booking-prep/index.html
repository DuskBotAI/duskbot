turn a calendly booking into an opening question

skip the opening question your guest already answered when they booked. before your first intro call, have a bot read the booking and the invitee details in calendly.

by the dusk bot team · checked 20 november 2026 · how we made this

you get their stated goal, something to bring and a question that takes the conversation one step further.

the message

paste this into a chat with your bot

every day at 7 am local time, list my active calendly bookings from now until midnight. use only my one-on-one event type called "intro call".

read the active invitee details for each booking. bring the time, name and stated reason for booking back to this chat. suggest one thing to bring and one opening question. if the invitee left no reason, say so and ask what they want to cover.

skip cancelled bookings. never change a booking or contact a guest. stay quiet if the list loads and no intro calls qualify. if invitee details won't load, flag that beside the time.
when
daily at 7 am in the host’s time zone, for intro calls still ahead today
needs
calendly read only for your scheduled events and their invitees3. use your own event type name in the message.

what comes back

an example, not a real run

10:30 am · iris bell · intro call
booking answer: wants to import a supplier list and keep the existing item codes.
bring: a sample import with an item-code column.
open with: do any suppliers use the same code for different items?

make it yours

  • pick the event by name. use the exact booking type clients choose. leave office hours and group sessions out.
  • keep the guest’s words. ask for a short excerpt if a paraphrase loses the technical detail.
  • move prep earlier. for breakfast calls, schedule the routine the evening before and change the window to tomorrow.

when it goes wrong

  • a guest appears twice after rescheduling. read the current event and invitee status. keep only the active booking.
  • the brief invents a job title. omit job titles unless the invitee actually gave one. a company email address tells you too little.

use the intake answer to choose what to bring

a specific request earns a specific prop. if someone mentions item codes, bring a sample row with a code in it. leave the full product tour for another day.

if guests only pick a time and never answer intake questions, the booking itself may be all the prep you need.

questions

what if a call lands after the morning run?

ask for that booking in chat. the morning list reflects what calendly held at 7 am. a later booking needs a fresh read.

can i leave phone numbers out of the brief?

yes. ask for the guest’s name, time and goal only. there is no reason to copy their contact details into the prep note.
