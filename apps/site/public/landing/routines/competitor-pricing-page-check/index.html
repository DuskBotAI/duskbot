spot a real price change on a competitor’s pricing page

an annual discount can look like a price cut if the billing toggle moves. give your bot one public pricing page and a price you have seen yourself.

by the dusk bot team · checked 20 november 2026 · how we made this

on its own browser it can compare the same offer each week. the note in chat tells you which figure moved or why the two offers no longer match.

the message

paste this into a chat with your bot

every monday at 10 am, open https://finchpaper.example/pricing in your own browser. baseline (nov 9): studio plan, uk view, monthly billing, gbp 18 per seat per month.

read that plan with those same terms. you may switch the public billing toggle. if the amount changes, report the baseline and current figure here with the terms, url and observation date.

stay quiet on a confirmed match. if the page won't load, the plan disappears or you can't match the terms, tell me which part you couldn't compare. never sign in, start checkout, submit a form or choose a substitute tier.
when
mondays at 10 am
needs
the bot’s own browser, a public pricing url and your dated baseline. no connected app.2 use your pricing url and dated baseline in the message.

what comes back

an example, not a real run

finch paper studio · pricing observed nov 16
finchpaper.example/pricing · studio plan · uk · monthly billing · per seat
nov 9 baseline: gbp 18 per month. current page: gbp 21 per month.
both views show the monthly option. difference: gbp 3 per seat per month.

make it yours

  • name the exact price line. “from” and “for the first month” can sit beside a regular price. pick the one you would pay.
  • keep one country view. supply a regional page address if the site has one. leave taxes out of comparisons unless both prices say how they treat tax.
  • edit the routine with the new dated baseline once you confirm the change. otherwise the old figure will keep triggering the note.

when it goes wrong

  • the old plan name vanished. record the missing label. decide yourself whether a new tier offers a fair comparison.
  • the page shows a monthly equivalent. read the text beside the amount. “per month, billed annually” belongs to the annual offer.

decide what counts as the same offer

write down the words around the number. include “per seat” if it appears. a flat team price and a seat price can use the same currency symbol and mean very different things.

if your decision depends on a negotiated rate, use the quote or contract. a bot reading a public pricing page cannot tell you what your account pays.

questions

will i hear about a change the moment it happens?

you get a scheduled observation. an offer can change and change back between monday visits. nothing in that comparison dates the change itself.

what if my computer sleeps on monday morning?

a local routine waits while the computer sleeps. if it missed the run by less than 12 hours, it runs once when you return. it skips older runs. dusk cloud runs on time.1
