routines to copy

a routine is a job a bot does on a schedule, or when something happens. each one here is a message you paste into a chat.

routines

  • turn a calendly booking into an opening questionstart where the booking form left off.
  • spot a real price change on a competitor’s pricing pagehold the terms steady before comparing prices.
  • sort a dropbox inbox without guessing where files belongsee both paths before a file moves.
  • remember what you finished before friday slips awaykeep the evidence underneath each win.
  • triage a github issue when it opensa new report, with context for a maintainer.
  • see which github pull requests still need your reviewthe reviews that still have your name on them.
  • see what is holding up open gitlab merge requestssee which change needs another pair of eyes.
  • draft the weekly report from the work log in google docslet dated notes carry the weekly report.
  • find open hubspot deals nobody has updated in two weekstake a specific question to pipeline review.
  • find intercom customers still waiting for a human replya private note leaves the customer waiting.
  • recap a linear cycle before the sprint closesbring the open decision to sprint review.
  • give a mailchimp campaign a day before reading the resultsread each figure with its campaign and time.
  • meeting prep briefs for outside calls, every weekday at 8the last conversation, before the next one.
  • turn promises in notion meeting notes into tasksgive each task a line in the minutes.
  • find a product mention buried in a reddit commentfind the words that made a thread worth opening.
  • a morning digest of unresolved sentry errorsone issue can explain a noisy night.
  • flag low shopify stock where the order gets packedcount the shelf that fills the order.
  • an end-of-day digest of the slack channels you chooseread the decisions, then follow the thread.
  • which of yesterday's failed stripe payments still need attention?a failed attempt can end in a paid order.
  • pick tomorrow’s first todoist task tonightone first move for tomorrow morning.
  • pull overdue trello cards into the morning standupgive an overdue card a place in standup.
  • read yesterday’s typeform answers in one short summarycount the blanks beside the answers.
  • a sunday evening preview of next week's calendarclashes and crunch days, flagged every sunday.
  • match weekly receipts to the sheet you already keepfind the gaps before updating your records.
  • which urgent zendesk tickets still need an owner?start the shift with unclaimed urgent work.
