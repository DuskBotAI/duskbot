give a mailchimp campaign a day before reading the results

give a newsletter time to collect clicks before you judge it. each morning, a bot reads mailchimp campaigns sent two calendar days ago to the audience you choose.

by the dusk bot team · checked 20 november 2026 · how we made this

you get delivery and engagement figures side by side in chat. each line names the metric so repeat clicks never masquerade as extra readers.

the message

paste this into a chat with your bot

daily at 9 am, list the sent campaigns from two calendar days ago for the mailchimp audience "winter glaze list". use my local time for the date boundaries. read each matching campaign report.

bring the campaign title, send time and report time into this chat. give emails sent, hard and soft bounces, unique opens and unique clicks when available. label each figure exactly. mark an absent field as unavailable. use zero only when the report actually gives zero.

never change an audience, resend a campaign or look up individual subscribers. stay quiet when the complete list has no qualifying campaign. flag a campaign whose report you couldn't read.
when
9 am every day for campaigns sent on the calendar date two days earlier
needs
mailchimp read access to the campaign list and campaign reports for the chosen audience3. put your audience name in the message.

what comes back

an example, not a real run

marta’s kiln notes · winter glaze list
sent tuesday 2 pm · report read thursday 9 am
emails sent 240 · hard bounces 2 · soft bounces 1
unique opens 88 · unique clicks 19

make it yours

  • pick a single audience. a wholesale circular and a public newsletter serve different readers. keep their results apart.
  • wait longer for a slow newsletter. change the window to campaigns from seven days ago if subscribers read over the weekend.
  • keep the comparison fair. compare campaigns at roughly the same age. include the send and read times with both.

when it goes wrong

  • a draft looks like a campaign nobody opened. restrict the list to sent campaigns. the report action can return zero figures for drafts.3
  • clicks turn into buyers in the recap. call them clicks. a click by itself tells you nothing about an order.

read delivery before interpreting engagement

start with how many emails went out and how many bounced. then look at unique clicks. total clicks can include several from the same reader. keep the labels beside the counts when you copy them into a weekly note.

for a single newsletter you only review once, opening its mailchimp report directly is simpler.

questions

are these the final campaign numbers?

treat them as a reading at the stated time. a subscriber can interact later. ask for another report on the same campaign if the later figures matter.

does reporting need permission to send campaigns?

read only is enough for this report. a separate send asks first by default.
