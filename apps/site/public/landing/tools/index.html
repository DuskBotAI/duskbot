what the calculator works out

keeping a bot running costs less in power than in your time. the always-on cost calculator puts four places side by side: your own laptop, a spare computer at home, a rented server and dusk cloud.

you type six numbers:

  • how many hours a day bots should run
  • your electricity price, from your last bill
  • what the spare computer draws when idle
  • your server's monthly price
  • the hours a month you'd spend on upkeep
  • what an hour of your time is worth

it then shows a month of each, with your time counted. dusk bot is free to run on your own computer or your own server. dusk cloud is a flat $20 a month on plus or $50 on pro, and we do the upkeep (pricing).

every sum runs in your browser, and the page shows the arithmetic one step per line, so you can check it against your own bill.
