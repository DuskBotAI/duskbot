dusk bot vs grok bot for a month of use

if you already pay for ai and mostly work at your desk, dusk bot adds no personal desktop fee. if you already have x premium, grok bot includes a cloud computer in that bill.

by the dusk bot team · checked 20 november 2026 · how we made this

the difference shows up when you hit a limit, switch phones or want your files back.1234

the short version

pick grok bot if

  • you already use the x bundle. try its included computer before you buy hosting elsewhere.15
  • you rely on a native android app. grok bot has one and can finish cloud work after your phone goes away.2

pick dusk bot if

  • you want your notes on your own disk. choose the desktop and decide which ai handles each bot.67
  • you want different models for different jobs. changing one bot's model leaves its files and memory in place.7

from sign-in to cancellation

follow a chat from the device in your hand to the machine that keeps it. these choices affect what you can still reach at the end of the month.

a light month at your own desk

compare the extra cost against what you already pay. suppose you use a bot occasionally for personal work. your current ai plan covers it, and your computer is awake when you need it. for grok, assume monthly premium and usage within the weekly pool.1412

dusk desktop: existing ai bill + $0 = existing ai bill
grok, new subscriber: $30 premium + $0 overage = $30
grok, already on premium: $30 total - $30 already budgeted = $0 extra

if you plan to keep premium for cursor anyway, switching the bot alone won't save that $30.

a heavy month with the laptop shut

price the computer and the extra ai separately. suppose you want a hosted machine for work. dusk pro includes a work license. on grok, compare a monthly tier such as ultra. neither price tells you how much your particular job will use.13412

dusk pro: $12 work license + $38 hosting = $50
month on dusk pro = $50 + your provider's ai charges
month on x ultra = $200 + prepaid credits used beyond the pool

ultra is an example, not a requirement for heavy use. grok publishes relative allowances but no fixed number of finished tasks. on dusk, a larger cloud machine does not buy more ai from your provider. use your own bill to fill in the missing amount.3412

the last day of the subscription

save anything you need before you give up access. a dusk backup leaves out credentials. restoring it replaces the current setup and brings routines back paused.6

on grok, downloading files and sharing a bot template serve different purposes. neither gives you a full chat archive.11 deleting the cursor account removes bots, chats and computers, with a purge within 30 days.2 use the migration steps if you want to keep the bot’s working setup.

how we checked

these examples show bills, not a speed test. we make dusk bot. the grok plan and app details come from sources checked on 20 november 2026. for each example we state what the person already pays for. neither company quotes a per-task price for the work in those examples.

questions

do i need a dusk subscription to use it on my phone?

you can pair your phone to the desktop for personal use. leave the computer on and dusk running. pair to dusk cloud instead if you need replies when the computer is off.410

is grok bot free if i have a cursor subscription?

eligible legacy cursor plans already include it. paid individual plans and self-serve teams qualify. usage beyond the weekly pool can still reach on-demand billing. see the pricing breakdown.212

free on your own computer, with every feature. no account, no card.
